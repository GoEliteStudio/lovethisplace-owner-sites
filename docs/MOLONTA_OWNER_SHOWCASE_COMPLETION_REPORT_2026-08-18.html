01 · Final operating decision

Ready to show, deliberately invisible to search engines.

The Molonta presentation is treated as an owner-authorized commercial showcase. It can be shared directly and used while LoveThisPlace searches for qualified rental business. It does not require a password, access code, login, or authentication gate.

Current publication boundary: anyone with the URL may view it, but the pages emit noindex, nofollow, the preview host blocks crawling through robots.txt, and Molonta is excluded from generated sitemap discovery. This gives Juan a frictionless presentation link without deliberately asking search engines to rank it yet.

04 · Design and conversion work

Molonta received its own visual system rather than a cosmetic copy of Casa Del Toro.

  • A refined heritage-led editorial design was created around the estate’s family history, Adriatic setting, architecture, hosts, and land.
  • The hero sequence begins with the estate at twilight and continues through the pool, coastline, sunset, yacht, interior, and night views.
  • The story section centers the Metković family, the house’s 1930s origin, Villa Palma history, and third-generation stewardship.
  • Desktop story and suite sections use controlled sticky layouts so long copy does not leave large dead areas.
  • The location module was changed from a decorative image into a compact, useful map presentation.
  • The amenities navigation now lands on a real section.
  • Rates, inclusions, available services, booking notes, and inquiry paths are separated clearly.
  • The FAQ was redesigned as a refined split accordion instead of a full-width wall of text.
  • The image viewer uses a stable centered frame so horizontal and vertical photographs remain aligned.
  • No invented testimonials or generic reviews are used.

The presentation can generate business without exposing unnecessary data.

  • Operational logs no longer print customer or owner email addresses.
  • No authentication screen or access-code form is present on the presentation.
  • Owner routing remains configuration-driven.
  • Raw source images and the owner-supplied archive are excluded from public assets and Git.
  • Only curated, resized, metadata-stripped delivery derivatives enter the public asset tree.
  • No secrets were found in the committed change set.
